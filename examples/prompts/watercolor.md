Transparent watercolor on ivory cold-pressed paper. Left 72% reconstructs the scene, right 28% is a quiet archive column divided by uniform circular perforations. Airy washes, reserved highlights and fine broken pen lines. Source-derived muted palette. Exact text: "LAKESIDE COTTAGE" and "NO. 01". No invented location/date, no source photograph, no heavy gouache.
